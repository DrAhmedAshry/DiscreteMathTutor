# Discrete Mathematics Tutor

A 24/7 tutor for **MATH-501 / 305 Discrete Mathematics**, German University
in Cairo — Dr. Ahmed Ashry.

It answers from this course's own material: the lecture slides, the practice
assignments and their solutions, and past exams and quizzes. **No answer is
generated by an AI at the moment a student asks.** Every one of the 366
answers was written in advance, and when nothing matches, the tutor says so
instead of inventing something.

Full solutions to practice exercises are not given on first request. A
student takes three hints, or posts an attempt, and then the answer opens.
