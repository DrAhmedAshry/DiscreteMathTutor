# How to use the Discrete Mathematics tutor

**MATH-501/305 · German University in Cairo**

This is a tutor, not an answer machine. Used well, it is the fastest help you can get at two in the morning. Used lazily, it will refuse you politely and you will have wasted your time.

Read this page once. It takes three minutes and will save you many.

---

## What it knows

Only this course. Your lecture slides, your practice assignments and their solutions, and past exams and quizzes. Nothing else.

That is the point. A general AI tool will answer confidently and sometimes contradict the conventions of this course — and you will lose marks for it. This tutor answers from the actual material you were taught.

---

## Three kinds of question it answers well

**1. About an idea.**

> Explain equivalence relations.
> Why does induction need a base case?
> What is the difference between a relation and a function?

Answered immediately and in full. Concepts are always free.

**2. About a specific exercise.**

> How do I approach PA07 Exercise 4?
> I am stuck on part (b) of PA05 Exercise 2.

Answered — but see "How it treats solutions" below.

**3. About studying and the course itself.**

> How should I prepare for Quiz 2?
> How much detail does the marking expect in a proof?
> Which topics are examinable this term?

## How to ask so you get a good answer

Compare these two:

| This works | This does not |
|---|---|
| "Explain how to prove a relation is transitive" | "i dont get lec 7 help" |
| "In PA06 Exercise 3, why is the empty set a subset?" | "sets??" |

Name the idea, or name the exercise. One sentence is enough. You do not need to be polite or formal — you need to be **specific**.

**Language.** Please write in English. Arabic and franco-Arabic will be accepted, but you will get an answer asking you to switch. Two reasons: academic English is a skill this degree expects of you, and an unclear question produces an unreliable answer.

## How it treats solutions

**It will not hand you the full solution to a practice assignment or exam question the first time you ask.**

This is deliberate. Reading a solution feels like learning and is not. The mark you get in the exam comes from what you can do with a blank page.

What happens instead:

1. You ask.
2. You get a hint — which idea applies.
3. Still stuck? A second hint — the method, named.
4. Still stuck? The first step, worked out.
5. Then the full solution, in your instructor's own wording.

You can also skip the ladder by **posting your attempt**. A few honest lines are enough. Show what you tried and the tutor will work from there — that is faster than the hints and you will learn more.

**Concepts, definitions and lecture material are never gated.** Only worked answers are.

---

## About exam questions

You can ask about any past exam question in the released range and see it in full.

**Model answers to exams are not published here.** Not to be difficult — the honest reason is that the solution files kept alongside old exams are incomplete and in places belong to questions that were later replaced. Showing them would mislead you, and being confidently wrong is worse than saying nothing.

So when you ask about an exam question, the tutor will show you the question, and then point you to **the practice assignment exercises that use the same ideas** — with their solutions, which were written to be published and are reliable.

Work that exercise. It is the same mathematics, it is trustworthy, and it is what the exam was testing.

## When it cannot help

Sometimes it will not have a good answer. It will say so rather than invent one, and show you the closest material it does have.

If none of that helps, there is a button to send your question to Dr. Ashry's review list. Use it. Questions collected there get proper answers added to the tutor, so the next student who asks is helped — often within the week.

---

## What it is not

- Not a replacement for lectures. It points back to them constantly, by slide number.
- Not a place to get your assignment done for you. It is built to prevent exactly that.
- Not marking you. Nothing you type here affects your grade.

---

## The short version

Ask specific questions. Try before you ask for answers. Post your attempt when you have one. Use it at two in the morning when nobody else is awake.

It is free, it is available always, and it knows this course. The effort is still yours.
